This policy covers the Ask D.L. SMS service (text a question to +1 888-823-1411) and the OurSpace website at https://ourspacevibes.com.

## What we collect

- **Your mobile phone number**, when you text the Ask D.L. number.
- **The text of the messages you send us**, and the replies we send back.
- Basic delivery metadata (time of message, delivery status) provided by our messaging carrier.

We do not ask for, and you should not send, passwords, payment card numbers, Social Security numbers, or other sensitive personal information by text.

## How we use it

- Your number and message text are used **only to answer your message** — one conversational reply per message you send — and to operate, secure, and rate-limit the service (currently capped at 6 messages per hour and 20 per day per sender).
- Message text is processed by an AI language-model provider solely to generate your reply.
- Delivery metadata is used to diagnose delivery problems and prevent abuse.

## What we never do

- We **do not sell** your phone number or message content.
- We **do not share** your phone number or message content with third parties for their marketing.
- We **do not send marketing blasts or outbound campaigns**. We only reply to messages you send first.

## Sharing necessary to run the service

Your messages pass through our messaging carrier (Twilio) and our AI processing provider for the sole purpose of delivering and generating replies. These providers process the data on our behalf and for no other purpose.

## Opting out

Reply **STOP** at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply **HELP** for help. Opting out is immediate and free of charge, apart from any standard messaging rates your own carrier charges you.

## Retention

Message logs are kept only as long as needed to operate the service, prevent abuse, and resolve delivery issues, and are then deleted or de-identified.

## Children

The Ask D.L. SMS service is intended for adults. It is separate from the Legacy Circle children's platform, which is never connected to this number.
